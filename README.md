## Selected projects

### Healthcare Operations Platform (HOP)
A multi-tenant HealthTech platform in active development and validation, designed around real clinic workflows: patient management, scheduling, clinical operations, CRM and administration. The work includes tenant isolation, role-based access and auditable processes; it is not presented as production-ready.

### Lisza AI
An independent product exploring professional scheduling, administrative CRM and AI-assisted communication workflows. The MVP is under development.

### MAIUS / Global Hair
A production-oriented institutional web experience for a healthcare and aesthetics business, with attention to content governance, SEO, accessibility and quality assurance.

### Tu Pedido
A full-stack commerce application focused on reliable order processing, inventory integrity, administrative workflows and transactional consistency.
